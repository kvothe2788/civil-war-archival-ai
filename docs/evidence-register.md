## Open questions

- Can the 1916 pontón be located on historic road plans and linked to the present hydrography?
- Did a small fall and pool exist near any of the candidate paths before later mining and road works?
- Can any independent testimony corroborate the reported exposure of remains after heavy rain, its date, and what happened afterwards?
- Which claims can be verified directly from records and which should remain unresolved?

No point in this register is an identified grave. A negative search result is not proof that no relevant record or grave exists.
